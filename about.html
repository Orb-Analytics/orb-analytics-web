Our Mission Statement

Our mission is to bring transparency to the sports gambling market. Using Python-powered predictive models, Orb delivers data-driven betting insights across the NFL, NBA, and MLB. Every pick, win, loss, and unit is tracked publicly.

Our Value Proposition

Staying informed means constant context-switching between scores on ESPN, headlines on Reddit, and lines and line movement scattered across third-party apps. Orb Analytics was built to distill the macro professional sports landscape into a single, vertically integrated platform.
