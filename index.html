Our mission is to bring transparency to the sports gambling market. Orb Analytics uses Python-powered models to produce betting insights based on probability and market edges across the NFL, NBA and MLB.

Staying informed usually means switching between scores on ESPN, headlines on Reddit, and lines scattered across third-party apps. Orb Analytics puts it in a single platform.

Orb Analytics only gives out games where our models have an edge over the market. Responsible gambling, to us, means wagering only where we have that edge.

Model edge is the gap between the win probability from our models and the market's. If the market has Team A to win at 52% and our model has Team A at 55%, our model edge is 3%.

Models can be wrong, and results vary.

Set a fixed wager size at the start of the season and take every pick the models give out. The goal is positive weeks, one after another. Winning a 3-2 week is a good outcome, and so is limiting losses in a 2-3 week. Slightly positive weeks strung together compound over time. The model currently delivers a 6.16% ROI.
